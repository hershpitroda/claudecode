# Facts

Every number shown or spoken in the film, with its source. The office itself is a model: no CAD files exist for it, so its layout and the floor plate size are not stated as facts, and no dimension of them appears on screen.

| Claim in the film | Shown as | Source | Quoted wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| Desk height range, seated to standing | 559–1,275 mm | Eureka Ergonomic, "BIFMA G1 Compliance" guide, citing ANSI/BIFMA G1-2013: https://eurekaergonomic.com/blogs/eureka-ergonomic-blog/bifma-g1-compliance-guide-public-offices | "Seated Desk Height \| 22.0 – 32.2 \| Inches" and "Standing Desk Height \| 37.2 – 50.2 \| Inches". 22.0 in = 558.8 mm → 559 mm; 50.2 in = 1,275.1 mm → 1,275 mm. |
| Chair seat height range | 376–512 mm | ANSI/BIFMA G1-2013 Ergonomics Guideline for Furniture, excerpt: https://www.scribd.com/document/929848178/Bifma-g1-Ergonomics-Guideline-for-Furniture | "have a minimum seat height range from 376 mm (14.8 in.) to 512 mm (20.2 in.)" |
| Adjustable seat depth range | 356–470 mm | Eureka Ergonomic guide (as above) | "Seat Depth (Adjustable) \| 14.0 – 18.5 \| Inches". 14.0 in = 355.6 mm → 356 mm; 18.5 in = 469.9 mm → 470 mm. |
| Knee clearance width under a desk | ≥ 508 mm | Eureka Ergonomic guide (as above) | "Knee Clearance Width \| 20.0 (Minimum) \| Inches". 20.0 in = 508 mm. |

Notes:

- The Eureka page is a secondary source quoting the guideline. The seat-height figure is quoted from an excerpt of the guideline itself. The full ANSI/BIFMA G1-2013 document is sold by BIFMA and was not read in full.
- The modelled furniture is built to sit inside these ranges: desks travel 559–1,275 mm, chair seats 376–512 mm.
